# Instructions for Filling Out Weekly Volunteer Report

## 📌 General Requirements

Each volunteer participates in the program based on the Volunteer Law ("Službeni glasnik RS", No. 36/2010).

According to the law, each volunteer is required to dedicate at least 10 hours per week to volunteer activities.

This is not an internal organization requirement, but an official legal norm.

The weekly report must contain at least 10 hours of work and be accompanied by proof of work performed — links, files, screenshots, etc.

If you continue a task from the previous week, be sure to show progress — what exactly was done additionally (new materials, results, number of hours, etc.).

---

## 📆 Vacation or Missing a Week for Valid Reasons

❗If you plan a vacation, trip, or know in advance that you will not be able to perform volunteer activities in a certain week:

- You must work extra hours in other weeks in advance (for example, complete more tasks before departure),
- Or make up the missing hours after returning.

📌 Each week of participation must be closed with a total of at least 10 hours.

📝 **Important**: you must inform and coordinate this in advance with Ingacheva Alla (Telegram: adolfovnaK email: rdsplatforma@gmail.com)

---

## 🏥 Sick Leave

⚖️ In accordance with the Volunteer Law ("Službeni glasnik RS", No. 36/2010), the concept of "sick leave" is not provided for volunteers as it applies to employees. Volunteer activity is voluntary and unpaid, and does not fall under labor legislation regulating paid leave or temporary disability.

However, the law requires the volunteer to notify the organizer of any circumstances that may:
- affect the performance of volunteer duties;
- create a threat to their life or health, as well as the life or health of other persons.

📢 In case of illness or other valid reason, you are required to promptly inform Ingacheva Alla (Telegram: adolfovnaK email: rdsplatforma@gmail.com) and agree on how the missed hours will be compensated (before or after).

## 📝 Instructions for Filling Out the Report Form

![Report Form](/resources/reporting-guide/new_report_EN.png)

### 📌 Block — Task Title

Specify a brief and clear task title.

**📌 Examples:**
- Translation of article about plastic recycling
- Garbage collection in Nova Daruga area
- Creating slides for presentation
- Writing a guide (article) on topic

You can add multiple tasks if you worked on different projects during the week.

![Adding a task](/resources/reporting-guide/new_task_EN.png)

### 📌 Block — Task Description

Describe what exactly you did within the task.

**📌 Examples:**
- Translated article from Serbian to Russian, edited text, checked style. Posted in Google Docs and sent to curator for review.
- Collected information about Russian-speaking NGOs in Georgia: names, contacts, addresses, websites.
- Tested bot in real mode, added test channels and news, checked different calendar versions.
- Found information on the internet about restaurants, church and winery, formatted data according to template. Discussed text with coordinator and added section about annual festivals held in Karlovci.

The more detailed the task description, the better. This helps the coordinator understand what exactly you did, assess the volume of work and correctly count the specified number of hours. Brief formulations like "translated article" or "participated in meeting" do not give a complete picture of the time and effort invested. Try to describe not only the result, but also the process of completing the task.

### 📌 Block — Result

Specify a link to the result if it is posted online:
- text materials — Google Docs / Drive
- code — GitHub
- visual materials — Canva, Figma, Google Drive

### 📌 Block — Number of Hours

Specify how much time you actually spent on completing the task.

### 📌 Block — Completion Date

Specify the date or actual period of task completion. If the task was performed on different days, you need to split it into separate tasks by days and specify exact completion dates for each.

### 📌 Block — Client

If the task was assigned by a coordinator or team member, specify their name.

If the task was performed on your own initiative, this field can be left empty.

### 📌 Block — File Attachments

If you have screenshots, photos, PDF documents and other supporting materials — attach them to the task.

- You can attach up to 15 files.
- If there are more files, create a separate task and add the missing materials there.
- Maximum file size — 5 MB.
- If you conduct online classes, it is advisable to attach a photo of the class or lesson plan.

**📌 Examples:**
- Photo from cleanup site
- Article screenshot
- Translation file
