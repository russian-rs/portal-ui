# Clean City (Main Program)

### Program Description

The **"Clean City"** program is an initiative for those who want to make their environment cleaner and more pleasant.
The program aims to support the ecological situation in Serbian cities through regular cleanups of public spaces.
Through the joint efforts of participants, we create a comfortable and safe environment for ourselves, our children, and
everyone around us.

Volunteers in this program are required to clean selected public areas weekly, dedicating at least **10 hours per week**
to this activity.

---

### Responsibilities of Volunteers in the "Clean City" Program

1. **Choosing a Cleanup Area**: Volunteers independently select an area for cleanup, preferably near their place of
   residence or in locations they are familiar with and where they notice garbage accumulation.
   This can be a park, square, roadside, playground, or any other public space.

2. **Defining Cleanup Boundaries**: Clearly outline the boundaries of the chosen area to effectively plan the work and
   assess the volume of upcoming tasks.

3. **Assessing the Amount and Type of Waste**: Before starting cleanup, visually estimate the volume and type of waste.
   If separate waste collection is planned, prepare appropriate bags for different types of waste (plastic, glass,
   paper, etc.).

4. **Photographing the Process**: It is mandatory to take photos **before, during, and after** the cleanup. Capture the
   overall view of the area before starting, the process of collecting garbage, the gathered bags, and the final result
   after cleaning. Photos serve as proof of the completed work.

5. **Weekly Reporting**: Each week, volunteers must submit a report on their work on the volunteer portal.

---

## Cleanup Guidelines for Volunteers in the "Clean City" Program

### Safety Measures During Cleanup:

- Before starting, **wear gloves and comfortable closed shoes**.
- Be cautious when handling **broken glass, rusty metal, and other hazardous items**.
- When working **near roads or water bodies**, take safety precautions and avoid dangerous areas.
- In hot weather, **stay hydrated** and take breaks to prevent overheating.

### Waste Collection and Disposal:

- Use **durable garbage bags** and tie them securely to prevent waste from being scattered by the wind.
- After cleanup, **leave the bags in designated areas** for municipal services to collect.
- If possible, **separate waste**: collect plastic, glass, and paper separately for recycling.

Following these guidelines will make the cleanup process **efficient, safe, and convenient** for all participants. **A
clean city is everyone's responsibility!**

## Obtaining Supplies

If you are unable to purchase **bags, gloves, or other necessary items**, the organization is ready to provide them. To
do so, please **contact the coordinator in advance** to arrange a time and place for pickup.

## Instructions for Submitting Reports on the "Clean City" Volunteer Portal

All volunteers participate in the program under the **Volunteer Law
** ([Službeni glasnik RS, No. 36/2010](https://russian.rs/laws/zakon-o-volonterstve/)). Each volunteer must dedicate at
least **10 hours per week** to volunteer activities. You can distribute this time as follows:

- Perform a **large cleanup in one day** (e.g., in a park or along a specific street).
- Divide cleanup **over several days** in different locations (e.g., near home or at a playground).
- Other options coordinated with the supervisor (e.g., **maintaining cleanliness in a specific area**).

It is essential that the total adds up to **at least 10 hours of volunteer work per week**.

## How to Fill Out the Weekly Report?

To submit your weekly report, you need to:

1. **Log in** to the volunteer portal.
2. Select the **"New Report"** tab on the left menu panel.
3. Fill in all required fields of the form and **attach the necessary photos**.

### Required Fields:

1. **Title**: Enter the task title in the format:

    - _"Cleanup of [specify location]"_.
    - **Example**: _"Cleanup of Kalemegdan Park"_, _"Cleanup of Kneza Mihaila Street"_.

2. **Description**: Briefly describe what you did. Specify what was cleaned (e.g., plastic, paper, glass, leaves, etc.)
   and whether waste sorting was applied.

    - **Example**: _"Garbage and dry leaves cleaned near the Russian school", "Collected plastic, glass, and paper,
      swept the paths, removed branches. Used separate waste collection."_

3. **Result**: If you have a link to materials (e.g., a report in Google Docs or a publication), add it here. If no
   links are available, leave the field blank.

4. **Hours Spent**: Indicate the actual time spent on cleanup. If cleanup was conducted in multiple locations, create
   separate tasks for each location, specifying the time spent.

5. **Date**: Enter the actual date of the cleanup.

6. **Requester**: Fill in only if the cleanup was requested by someone.

7. **Files**: Attach at least **10 photos**.
    - **Mandatory photos:**
        - **Before cleanup** (showing the polluted area).
        - **After cleanup** (from the same angle to show the difference).
        - **During cleanup**.
        - **Collected garbage bags**.
        - **Google Maps screenshot** marking the cleanup area.

After filling in all fields, **click "Submit"**.

## Requirements for Photos and Information in Reports

Photos must clearly demonstrate:

- **The cleanup process**.
- **Results before and after**.
- **Time spent in accordance with the report**.
- **Clear and informative visuals**.

When taking photos, ensure that no bystanders, strangers, or children are in the frame. This is necessary to comply with
privacy and safety rules. If avoiding people in the shot is impossible, choose a different angle or take the photo when
no one is around.

Below are examples of correct and incorrect photos for submitted reports. Use them as a reference to avoid mistakes and
successfully confirm your volunteer work.

### **Correct Photos:**

- **Before and after cleanup from the same angle**.
- Optional - **cleanup process photos**.
- **Collected garbage bags** at the end of cleanup.
- **Google Maps screenshot** with the marked cleanup zone.

### **Incorrect Photos:**

- **Only a clean area** without proof of cleanup.
- **A small cleaned area** when a large amount of work time is reported.
- **Photos taken at night**.
- **Reused old photos**.

If an insufficient number of photos is attached, **the report may be rejected**.
